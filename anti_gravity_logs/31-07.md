### Key Analysis Highlights
1. **Transfer Learning (Recommended)**: Leveraging pre-trained encoders (e.g., ResNet-34 or EfficientNet-B3) using `segmentation-models-pytorch` (SMP) is highly superior to training from scratch with huge epoch times, as it uses features learned from millions of images and converges in just 15–20 epochs without overfitting on the small 707 training images.
2. **Spatially-sparse CNNs**: Benjamin Graham's Spatially-sparse CNNs are designed for physically sparse coordinates (like 3D point clouds or handwriting strokes on blank canvas). GONG solar disk observations are dense 2D images filled with complex solar textures. Therefore, standard dense pretrained U-Net / U-Net++ architectures are the correct choice.
3. **Data-Driven Post-Processing**: Annotation data analysis reveals that the 5th percentile of real filament area is **316.9 pixels** at full $2048 \times 2048$ resolution. Raising the connected components filter size from `30` to `300` pixels will filter out massive amounts of small noise, boosting precision.
4. **Enhanced Augmentations & Preprocessing**: Incorporating CLAHE for local contrast equalization and a complete `albumentations` pipeline (`ShiftScaleRotate`, `RandomBrightnessContrast`, `GaussNoise`).
